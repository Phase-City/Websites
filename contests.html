Live Game Show

The Big Quiz

Phase City's premier live game show — hosted in Discord, episode by episode. Questions cover city lore, server rules, community history, and general knowledge. Hosted by staff with live prizes for top scorers.

🎯
The Format

Multi-round quiz held live in Discord voice. Hosted by staff. Players submit answers in real time. Wrong answer = eliminated. Last player standing takes the episode.

📚
Question Categories

Phase City lore and history · Server rules and guidelines · Community moments and events · General knowledge · Wildcard rounds decided by the host.

🏆
Episode Prizes

Episode winner takes in-game cash prize. Series points awarded for top finishers each episode. Series champion crowned at season end with a special prize package.

📅
Schedule

Episodes announced in Discord with at least 48 hours notice. Follow #quiz-announcements to never miss an episode. Anyone whitelisted can enter.

NLRNew Life Rule
▾
NLR.1
If your character is downed and you choose to respawn at the hospital, your character has no memory of the events 15 minutes leading up to their death. You cannot reference who downed you, why, or where it happened.
NLR.2
You may not return to the scene of your death for 15 minutes after respawning, and may not seek revenge on the parties involved based on pre-death knowledge.
NLR.3
You may not respawn if advised via /ooc or by a nearby player that police or EMS are actively en route to your location. You must wait for medical RP to play out.
NLR.4
If revived by EMS, NLR does not apply unless downed fully in combat (gang interaction, player-to-player engagement). You must still realistically roleplay injuries following revival.
🚗
VDMVehicle Deathmatch
▾
VDM.1
VDM is the act of intentionally using a vehicle as a weapon against another player without valid RP initiation. Treated with equal severity to RDM.
VDM.2
Deliberate ramming, running over, or striking players or other vehicles with intent to harm — without an established RP reason — is VDM. This includes high-speed vehicle-to-vehicle ramming (100+ km/h).
VDM.3
The only acceptable exception is when using a vehicle is your only viable means of escape from someone actively threatening your life with a deadly weapon and you cannot go around them.
VDM.4
Ramp jumping, launching vehicles off bridges or inclines and continuing to drive as though undamaged is also a violation. Vehicles must be roleplayed realistically.

DOWNDowned / Incapacitated Players
▾
DOWN.1
While downed, you may not use your phone, radio, or any communication tool to relay information to allies — including your location, who shot you, or the status of a scene.
DOWN.2
Audible proximity responses (moaning, groaning, shallow breathing) are permitted and encouraged for immersion. Organized communication over radio or external voice is not.
DOWN.3
You may not use hand signals, mic clicks, or any coded method to communicate your situation to nearby players while incapacitated.
DOWN.4
If you were downed during an active scenario, you are considered out of that scenario upon revival or respawn. You may not re-enter the same scene that resulted in your incapacitation.

Whitelisted Gang Activity · Phase City

GANG HANDBOOK

This handbook governs all whitelisted gang activity on Phase City. It supplements the main server ruleset — it does not replace it. All standard server rules still apply. Where this handbook is more specific, this handbook takes precedence for gang-related matters.

🔱 Standards & Structure

Gang Rules

Gang RP is one of the most influential and visible forms of roleplay on the server. With that influence comes responsibility.

📖
Part 1Philosophy
▾
What Gang RP Is
Gang RP is about building a living, breathing criminal organization with culture, hierarchy, history, and consequence. It is about the story — the rivalries, the politics, the internal drama, the come-up and the fall. Done well, it creates some of the most compelling and memorable RP on the server for everyone involved, not just your crew.
What Gang RP Is Not
Gang RP is not a license to shoot. It is not a competitive team deathmatch mode with extra steps. If your gang's primary activity is running around the map looking for people to slide on, this is not the server for you.
✕Gangs that consistently produce low-quality, shoot-first RP will have their whitelist reviewed and revoked. There is no quota for violence. There is a standard for storytelling.
The Standard
Every hostile action your gang takes must be backed by genuine in-character reasoning. Every war must be earned through escalating conflict. Every slide must have a story behind it. Staff can and will ask you to justify your actions — "they were on our turf" is not always enough. Build the narrative.
📋
Part 2Whitelist & Application
▾
2.1
Becoming Official: Unwhitelisted groups may exist as criminal crews but do not receive territory recognition, cannot formally declare war, and have no standing with staff as an organized faction. To be recognized as an official gang, you must apply.
2.2
Application Requirements:
  • Minimum of 6 confirmed founding members who are active on the server
  • A named leader and at least one named second-in-command on record with staff
  • A gang name that does not reference real-world criminal organizations, political groups, or religious bodies
  • A written lore submission — background of your gang's origins, culture, territory interests, and current goals
  • A Discord ticket submitted through the appropriate channel
2.3
Probationary Period: All newly whitelisted gangs enter a 14-day probationary period. Rule violations during probation carry heavier consequences and may result in immediate whitelist removal.
2.4
Roster Management: Rosters must be updated within 48 hours of any membership change. A deliberately falsified roster is grounds for a gang strike.
2.5
Inactivity: A gang that falls below 6 active members for more than 14 consecutive days without staff-approved leave is considered inactive and subject to whitelist removal.
👥
Part 3Membership Tiers
▾
3.1
Hangarounds / Prospects:
  • Do not count toward official gang capacity numbers
  • May not participate in formal beefs, declared wars, or defensive castle scenarios
  • May not be used as additional bodies in hostile situations
  • Cannot hold a leadership role or speak on behalf of the gang
  • Hangaround period is 7 days minimum with at least 48 hours IC playtime
✕Hangarounds brought into conflicts anyway are a violation of the spirit of this rule even if the numbers technically work out.
3.2
Full Members: Formally patched in. Must have been on the server for a minimum of 48 hours and completed the 7-day hangaround period in RP.
⚠Bleeding out a member puts that player on a 7-day cooldown before they may become a hangaround for another gang/group.
3.3
Leadership Responsibility: Gang leaders and command staff are accountable for the conduct of their members. Repeated issues from the same gang without internal accountability will result in leadership receiving strikes alongside the offending members.
🏙️
Part 4Territory
▾
4.1
What Territory Means: Owning territory is a narrative anchor — your home, your block, your operation. It is not a mechanical kill zone. The rules exist to protect that story, not to give you license to gun down anyone who walks past.
4.2
Claiming Territory: Territory must be approved by staff at the time of your gang's whitelist application. Claims should be realistic in scope. You may not unilaterally expand your claimed territory without submitting an updated request and receiving approval.
4.3
Castle Defense: If a rival gang enters your approved territory and engages in threatening behavior, you may defend it. Castle defense allows the defensive gang to field up to 6 members with no additional spotters.
ℹA civilian walking through your block is not a target. A courier making a delivery is not a target. Engage with context and read the room.
4.4
Territory Disputes: Territory disputes must be resolved through RP escalation — not a single shootout. Let it build. Use it as a story.
4.5
Tagging:
  • You may only spray your own gang's tags — not impersonate another gang's markings
  • Removing or defacing another gang's tags is a recognized act of disrespect and a valid beef trigger
  • Tagging is not permitted in Green Zones
  • Do not use tagging as a mechanical bait tool
🎨
Part 5Colors & Identification
▾
5.1
Wearing Your Colors: Full members must wear identifiable gang colors, clothing, or markings when engaging in gang-representative activities — including beef, turf defense, war, and representing your gang in public.
5.2
Going Dark: Masks and plain clothes are permitted for heists and operations where disguise makes narrative sense. You cannot go dark and then claim gang retaliation rights in the same incident.
5.3
Impersonation: Impersonating another gang — wearing their colors, tagging their marks, or acting in their name without authorization — is prohibited. Impersonation as a deliberate RP tactic to frame another gang requires staff discussion before being executed.
5.4
Group Initiation via Colors: When a gang member wearing identifiable colors initiates on a rival, all members of both gangs are considered mutually initiated for the duration of that scenario. Masks, bags, shoes, and accessories alone do not constitute identifiable colors.
⚔️
Part 6Beef, Slides & Conflict
▾
6.1
What Is Beef: Beef is an active, escalating in-character conflict. It is the accumulation of disrespect, theft, territorial aggression, and confrontation that organically builds tension. It is the story before the war.
6.2
Valid Beef Triggers:
  • Repeated robbery of a gang's members or traps
  • Disrespect of a gang's leadership or set in public spaces
  • Defacing or claiming over a gang's territory tags
  • Moving into claimed territory without permission or negotiation
  • Attacking a business or operation under a gang's protection
  • Breaking a deal, betraying an alliance, or double-crossing a crew
  • Drive-bys or shots fired at a gang's home turf
  • Public social media disses (in-city platforms)
6.3
Invalid Beef Escalation:
  • A single minor interaction, argument, or fender bender
  • OOC dislike of another player or gang
  • Wanting their territory for mechanical/economic reasons with no RP buildup
  • Never having met or interacted with the other gang at all
6.4
The Slide Timer: Once a hostile scene has concluded — 5 minutes of no active shooting — a 30-minute cooldown timer begins before either party may initiate another slide.
✕During cooldown you may not re-initiate the same parties, drive through their territory antagonizing, or remove their tags knowing they cannot respond.
6.5
New Member Lockout: Any player who joins a gang has a 7-day lockout period before they may participate in active beefs or wars as an aggressor.
6.6
No Conflict at Community Events: Gang conflicts may not be brought to server-wide community events. Deliberately attending an event to bait or ambush a rival gang is prohibited and subject to a gang strike.
🏴
Part 7War
▾
7.1
What War Means: A formal gang war is a declared, staff-acknowledged state of escalated conflict. War should be the culmination of weeks of escalating beef, failed negotiations, and unresolved conflict — not a quick declaration.
7.2
War Declaration Requirements:
  • A documented history of in-character conflict (staff-observed incidents, ticket references, or written summary submitted to staff)
  • At least three distinct hostile incidents over a period of no less than 7 days
  • Both gang leaders must acknowledge the war declaration — a war cannot be unilaterally imposed
7.3
War Terms:
  • Duration — maximum of 14 days. Extensions require fresh staff review
  • Resolution conditions — what ends the war (decisive battle, negotiated settlement, staff-mandated ceasefire)
  • Dress code — all participants must wear identifiable colors at all times during conflict. No going dark during war scenarios
7.4
Allied Involvement: Other gangs may assist a warring party. Allied gang members still count toward the applicable group cap. Calling in allies does not increase your numbers beyond what the rules permit.
7.5
War Cooldown: Following resolution of a declared war, a 14-day ceasefire period begins. Standard beef interactions may resume after 7 days.
🤝
Part 8Alliances & Criminal Networks
▾
8.1
Alliances: Gangs may form in-character alliances. Alliances must be declared to staff and logged on record. An alliance allows allied members to assist in hostile scenarios subject to the group cap — it does not stack numbers above the cap.
8.4
No Coordinated Mega-Groups: No arrangement — formal alliance, informal understanding, or otherwise — may be used to field more than the maximum group cap in a hostile scenario. If your alliance effectively creates a 10-man squad going to war, it will be treated as rules circumvention.
🎯
Part 9Criminal Activity Standards
▾
9.1
RP Before Violence: Gang members are held to the same — and arguably a higher — standard of RP initiation as any other player. The fact that you are a whitelisted gang does not shorten the initiation requirement.
9.2
Don't Be a Farm: Gang activities should be driven by RP, not optimized purely for economy. Running the same loop — trap, rob, trap, rob — with no narrative and no engagement with the world around you is low-quality RP. Staff will flag gangs whose activity consists entirely of economic grinding.
9.3
Engaging the Wider City: The best gang RP touches other parts of the server. Your gang has a reputation — build it through interaction, not just body counts. Be a source of RP for the server, not just for yourselves.
9.4
Hitman Actions: A hit cannot be executed on someone who has had zero prior interaction, conflict, or warning with your gang or your client. Hits with no RP foundation are treated as RDM.

Part 10Gang Strikes & Discipline
▾
10.1
The Strike System:
StrikesConsequence
1Formal documented warning issued to leadership
2Suspension of war rights for 14 days; leadership meeting with staff required
3Gang whitelist removal
10.2
What Earns a Strike:
  • Multiple rule violations in a short period involving the same members
  • Consistent slides with no RP justification across multiple sessions
  • Violating war terms or declaring war without proper escalation
  • Roster fraud — deliberately inaccurate member lists
  • Leadership failing to act after being informed of a problem member
  • OOC toxicity between gang leadership and other gangs or staff
  • Using hangarounds, burner characters, or alts to pad numbers
10.3
Whitelist Removal: Your gang loses all official recognition, territory claims, war standing, and gang-specific rule protections. Members may continue to play — they revert to unwhitelisted criminal crew status. Re-application allowed after 30 days.
10.4
Leadership Changes Under Removal: If a gang is removed due to leadership failures specifically, the remaining members may petition staff to restructure under new leadership and re-apply without waiting the 30-day period. Granted at staff discretion.

Part 11OOC Standards for Gang Members
▾
11.1
Represent Your Set Well: You are a representative of your gang in every interaction — IC and OOC. Toxic behavior in Discord, spiteful tickets, trash-talking in OOC channels, and bad-faith reports against rival gangs all reflect on your crew. Gangs with chronic OOC toxicity issues will earn strikes regardless of how clean their in-game behavior is.
11.2
Beef Stays IC: Conflict between gangs is in-character. It ends when you close the game. Any OOC extension of IC gang beef will result in an automatic gang strike and personal warning points.
11.3
Accept the L: Losing a fight, losing territory, losing a war — these are story moments. Some of the best RP in any server's history came from the losing side. If you cannot take a loss gracefully, gang RP is not the right space for you.
11.4
Staff Relations: Gang leadership is expected to have a professional and cooperative relationship with staff. Raise disputes through proper channels, respectfully, after the scene. Arguing with staff in-city during active RP is not acceptable.

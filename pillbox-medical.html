Mission Statement

Phase City EMS (PCEMS) is dedicated to serving the residents of Los Santos and Blaine County. Our mission is to deliver prompt, professional, and compassionate emergency medical care to every individual — regardless of circumstance.

"We look after each other."

Core Values
  • Compassion — Dedicated to every patient, working diligently to alleviate pain and provide appropriate treatment.
  • Empathy — Genuinely caring about patients and families, working tirelessly toward positive outcomes.
  • Teamwork — Unified, well-coordinated organisation. Teamwork is the foundation of our growth.
  • Innovation — Continuously seeking ways to enhance and improve emergency medical services.
  • Safety — Reducing injury and errors through training, education, and proper protective equipment.
  • Honesty — Truthful and transparent with civilians, law enforcement, and colleagues at all times.
⚠ Members of PCEMS are prohibited from breaking any city laws. Zero-tolerance policy. Violations may result in immediate termination.

Disciplinary Actions
  • Verbal Warning — Issued for minor first-time infractions. Formal notice to correct behaviour.
  • Strike — Issued after a verbal warning. One warning permitted before strikes apply. Three strikes = termination.
  • Termination — Three strikes, or blatant violation of city laws, chain of command, or departmental regulations.
  • Blacklist — Permanent ban. Repeated departures, violent felonies, theft/sale of PCEMS property, or multiple major SOP violations within three months.

Code of Conduct
  • Treat all colleagues, superiors, and subordinates with respect at all times.
  • Treat all civilian bystanders and patients with courtesy and professionalism.
  • Cooperate with the LSPD, provided doing so does not place you in danger.
  • Follow the chain of command for all issues (see § 2.7).
  • Zero-tolerance on gossip, drama, and harassment of any kind — including sexual harassment.
ℹ PDA Policy: Public displays of affection at the hospital, in department vehicles, or in public while on duty are strictly prohibited. This includes riding together to calls.

General Operations
✏ Admin Edit
Standard Treatment Workflow
  • Introduce yourself to the patient.
  • Assess pain level on 0–10 scale (10 = worst). Pain ≥5 requires further diagnostic testing.
  • If transport required, verbally state you are packing wounds, then load patient into ambulance.
  • Below Doctor rank: all GSW and stab wounds must be transported to hospital.
  • Diagnose via third eye or F6 → lean over patient.
  • Perform X-rays via /e tablet2, CT scans, or MRIs as needed.
  • Treat patient using supplies from your medical bag.
  • Create a report if the case meets § 2.6 criteria. Bill via /billing.

EMS Neutrality

EMS is neutral. We do not provide information to law enforcement about criminal activity. Doing so endangers you and your colleagues.

  • Do not loiter at gang-affiliated locations while on duty.
  • Events at gang compounds require command approval and sufficient on-duty staffing.
  • Block parties are not covered by EMS.
⚠ If you are robbed while on duty, comply with all demands. Once the situation ends, clip the incident and report to staff. Do not compromise the scene while it is in progress.
Angel-1 Air Operations
✏ Admin Edit
Overview

Angel-1 is the call sign for the Pillbox Hospital medical helicopter. It brings the hospital to the patient when time is critical — around the clock, with rapid response cars available at night or in adverse weather.

A typical crew consists of an advanced trauma doctor, a paramedic, and a consultant. On-scene treatments include open-heart surgery, blood transfusions, and general anaesthetic administered roadside.

Crew Responsibilities
  • When double-crewed, the pilot does not participate in medical RP unless specifically needed.
  • The pilot remains with the aircraft at all times for security.
  • Pilots are responsible for the safety of the aircraft and all crew on board.
  • Maintain radio contact and provide relevant communications throughout the flight.
ℹ Lift Prerequisites: Angel-1 may only be lifted in suitable weather conditions and should be double-crewed whenever possible.
✏ Admin Edit
LZ Site Criteria
  • Terrain is level — do not land on steep slopes.
  • Area is as open as possible, giving maximum room to manoeuvre.
  • Nearby roads are closed or can be closed (request PD assistance if needed).
  • Site is clear of obstacles: poles, wires, signage, vehicles.
  • Large enough to safely accommodate the aircraft.
  • All ground personnel clear of the LZ before descent begins.
🚨 Unsuitable: No PD perimeter at multi-route intersections · Overhead power lines or sign gantries · Areas appearing clear at ground level but with wires overhead — always check above, not just at ground level.
Landing Procedure
  • Step 1: Request a cleared LZ. Coordinate with ground units while still in flight.
  • Step 2: Circle the site on approach to reduce speed and survey the area.
  • Step 3: Begin descent. Gradually lose altitude, position directly above the landing site.
  • Step 4: Touch down. Remain with aircraft until paramedic returns. If single-crewed, secure and lock before leaving.
  • Step 5: Use good judgement — a car park is always preferable to a busy road.

Department Structure
✏ Admin Edit
Promotion Criteria
  • Activity — Consistent engagement and experience-building beyond the minimum required hours.
  • Attitude — Positive, helpful demeanour toward civilians, colleagues, and LSPD.
  • Patient Care — Professional, empathetic treatment of all patients — physical and mental.
  • Documentation — Quality and thoroughness of reports. Going above minimum is noticed.
  • Professionalism — Communication skills, conduct, and reflection of department values.
Rank Tiers & Weekly Bonuses
Recruit · Base Bonus EMT · Mid Bonus Paramedic · Mid Bonus Doctor · Mid Bonus All Lead Positions · Senior Bonus
Probation Periods
  • Recruit Probation — Mandatory 5 days. Requires ≥5 hours shadowing a certified FTO plus FTO sign-off.
  • Paramedic → Doctor or higher — 1-month probationary period upon promotion.
  • Supervisor or higher — 2-month probationary period upon promotion.
  • Time spent on LOA does not count toward the probationary period.
✏ Admin Edit
Inactivity Policy
  • Inactive for one consecutive month without notifying leadership = risk of removal.
  • First removal for inactivity: one opportunity to return.
  • Second removal for inactivity: permanently removed, no option to return.
Leave of Absence (LOA)
  • LOA duration must not exceed one month at a time without leadership notification.
  • An estimated return date is required — "TBD" return dates will be denied.
  • 72-hour cooldown between LOA submissions.
  • Recruits are not eligible for LOA until training is complete.
Reinstatement
  • All returning members re-enter as EMT and complete refresher training.
  • Retirees returning within 4 months may return as Phase 3 EMTs.
  • Former leadership reinstated at Paramedic immediately following EMT refresher.
⛔ You may only be reinstated twice. A third removal results in a permanent blacklist from all PCEMS employment.
✏ Admin Edit
Legal Rights as EMS
  • You have the right to use the assigned district attorney for PCEMS-related legal questions.
  • You are protected under your sworn oath to assess situations and provide treatment to the best of your ability.
  • You have the right to pursue legal action against PCEMS if your termination was conducted under unreasonable or false pretences.
Medical Protection & Privacy Act (MPPA)

All patients have the legal right to have their sensitive personal health records and information protected and not disclosed without proper notification, a legal subpoena, or the patient's consent.

  • Medical records requested by the patient are to be provided by high command only.
  • Records requested by any other party must be obtained through a legal subpoena and processed by high command.

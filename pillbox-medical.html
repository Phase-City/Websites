01EMS Standards
▼
1.1 — Core Values
All members of PCEMS are required to uphold the values below at all times — from the highest to the lowest rank.
CompassionEmpathyTeamworkRespectHonestySafetyCommunityIntegrity
1.2 — Code of Conduct
Zero Tolerance Policy — PCEMS prohibits all forms of racism, sexism, harassment, and sexual harassment at all levels. Any breaches will result in immediate termination following an internal review.
Open Door Policy — If you have concerns, issues, questions, or recommendations, you may raise them with anyone regardless of their leadership level.
On-Duty Conduct
  • Conduct yourself with a high level of professionalism and maintain core values at all times.
  • Remain actively working. Brief rest periods permitted up to 30 minutes. If stepping away longer, please clock off duty.
  • Engage with the community — visit local businesses, hangout locations, and populated areas.
  • Avoid Personal Displays of Affection during working hours.
Social Media Usage — Social media activity is monitored. Disciplinary action may follow if usage is deemed to impact PCEMS or your duty to serve.
1.3 — Disciplinary Procedures
OutcomeStatusDescription
ANo Further ActionNo evidence of a breach, or insufficient evidence to proceed.
BVerbal WarningLow-level breaches. Issued verbally with no formal record.
CStrikeWritten warning logged on file for six months. Two strikes may result in demotion. Three or more within three months may lead to removal.
DDemotionIndicates unsuitability at current rank. Requires authorisation from Assistant Chief or Chief of EMS.
ERemoval from EMSLast resort. Applied only when continued trust is no longer possible.
FBlacklistPermanent ban. Includes serious felony convictions, organised crime links, or dangerous conduct.
1.4 — Off-Duty Conduct
  • Remain lawful and avoid committing any crimes.
  • Store all EMS equipment and tasers securely in your personal locker.
  • Do not treat patients or wear your EMS uniform off duty.
  • Avoid gang-affiliated clothing or criminal associations.
  • Avoid non-prescribed medication or illicit substances.
1.5 — Leave of Absence
ℹ If absent for more than one week, complete a Leave of Absence (LOA) form in your emails. LOAs exceeding one month may require a temporary rank change.
1.6 — Criminal Associations
All PCEMS employees must maintain a felony-free criminal history, verified monthly by Command with PCPD assistance. Report any criminal proceedings to the Assistant Chief or Chief of EMS immediately.
1.7 — Miscellaneous Rules
  • The sale of medical supplies is strictly prohibited — will result in PCPD involvement and full prosecution.
  • If robbed on duty, comply with demands, then report to police and preserve body camera footage.
  • All employees must remain on radio channel 2 while on duty.
  • Vehicles must be parked correctly within a bay in the garage when not in use.
  • Personal firearms are permitted but strictly prohibited while on duty — secure before your shift.
1.8 — Report Writing
A report is required after: any surgery, any DOA or in-hospital death (Chief + Government approval), any law enforcement altercation involving injury, and any lawsuit threat.
1.9 — Chain of Command
All questions and concerns should be directed to the next rank above you or the nearest available supervisor.
ℹ Example: If you are an EMT with a treatment question, speak to an A-EMT, S-EMT, or FTO first.
02Operating Procedures & Guidelines
▼
2.1 — Direct Orders
All direct orders must be followed unless doing so would result in an immediate threat to life, require breaking the law, or violate these SOPs. Report unlawful orders directly to High Command.
2.2 — Radio Usage
  • You must be on radio channel 2 when on duty. Breaches may result in a strike.
  • Conduct yourself professionally on the radio.
  • Do not join PD channel 1 without proper authorisation from PD Command.
  • If you are the only PCEMS staff on duty, inform PD at the start and end of your shift.
  • If PCPD requests EMS backup, wait for the highest-ranking medic to respond. Always request a location ping.

2.4 — Mandatory Radio Calls
  • When attaching to a dispatch call, announce your callsign and nearest postal code.
  • When a scene is cleared, announce callsign and call Code 4.
  • When transporting a patient, announce number of patients and pertinent details for in-house staff.
2.5 — Death
Permanent character deaths must be approved by the government via a ticket. Full MDT documentation required. High Command must be notified.
2.6 — Uniform Guidelines
  • Hats and glasses permitted if professional and not interfering with hygiene or treatment.
  • Watches and wristbands not permitted. A simple wedding band is allowed.
  • "Elbows and below to be clear" hygiene standard applies.
⚠ Command reserves the right to request removal of any non-compliant item. Non-compliance results in a verbal warning or strike.
03Patrolling & Hospital Operations
▼
3.1 — Patrolling and Stations
PCEMS operates one hospital across four districts. At least one medic must be at the hospital at all times. Priority system based on expected population:
#District
1Hospital
2Phase City
3Sandy Shores
4Paleto
5Grapeseed
ℹ Doctors should be stationed at the Hospital as priority. No more than two PCEMS members at the hospital at once.
3.2 — Ride Alongs
All ride-alongs must be approved by Command. You are responsible for the civilian's safety. Ensure no weapons, provide a vest, and drop them off at a safe location before any PCPD calls.
3.3 — Active Situations
  • Ensure all gunfire has ceased before entering an area. Never revive during an active situation.
  • If asked to leave a scene, do so immediately without argument.
  • If gunfire begins while on scene, leave immediately. Do not return for a minimum of 3 minutes.
⚠ Failure to comply with active-situation protocols will result in disciplinary action.
3.4 — Calls for PD Scenes
  • Check for downed officers first — they take priority.
  • Always ask: "Are they handcuffed?" before treating any suspect.
  • Ask officers for a situation briefing before treating the patient.
  • Before leaving, ask if anyone else requires medical attention.
3.5 — Code Silver — Hospital Lockdown
🔒 Code Silver
ℹ Active shooters in or within 500 feet of the hospital, or high-priority 10-95. "No new patients admitted. No non-EMS/PD personnel may enter until all-clear."
3.6 — Code Red — Mass Casualty
🔴 Code Red
ℹ In-house staff overwhelmed. All units complete current calls and cease responding to new calls until ER is cleared.
3.7 — Code Blue — Cardiac Arrest
💙 Code Blue
Patient has lost their pulse. Staff member calling Code Blue is assisted by the nearest colleague. One performs CPR, the other prepares the defibrillator.
04Gang Activity
▼
Gang activity has increased across the city. When encountering any gang activity, the following rules apply:
  • Wait a minimum of 2–3 minutes before entering the area to treat any 10-95 patients, or until authorised by EMS Command.
  • Do not discuss or report anything witnessed at gang-related scenes (e.g. drug operations, illegal activities).
  • EMS is neutral. We do not provide information to law enforcement about criminal activity.
  • Do not loiter at gang-affiliated locations while on duty.
  • Events at gang compounds require Command approval and sufficient on-duty staffing.

5.2 — Promotion Guidelines
  • Minimum of 3 weeks since last promotion.
  • Senior Staff: minimum 4-week gap.
  • Command: minimum 6-week gap.
  • High Command: must be approved by the Government.
  • EMT to A-EMT: approved by an FTO and Command member on completion of FTO sheet.
  • Senior Staff promotions require one departmental qualification.
  • Cannot have an active strike when being promoted into Command.
  • All promotions above A-EMT must be approved by High Command.
5.3 — Probationary Period
  • Paramedic to Doctor or higher: 1-month probationary period.
  • Promotion to Supervisor or higher: 2-month probationary period.
  • Time spent on LOA does not count toward the probationary period.
06Activity & Leave Policy
▼
6.1 — Inactivity
  • Inactive for one consecutive month without notice: at risk of removal.
  • First removal: given one opportunity to return.
  • Second removal: permanently removed with no option to return.
  • Paramedics and below reinstated at previous rank upon return.
  • Supervisors and above return at Paramedic or below, depending on departmental needs.
6.2 — Retiring
Retire at any time by informing a member of leadership. Retiring without notification forfeits your right to return as a rehire.
⚠ Leaving the EMS email without notifying High Command is treated as resignation — position and title will be removed.
07Law & Legal Notice
▼
7.1 — Legal Rights as EMS
  • Right to use the assigned district attorney for PCEMS-related legal questions.
  • Protected under your sworn oath to assess situations and provide medical treatment to the best of your ability.
  • Right to pursue legal action against PCEMS if termination was conducted under unreasonable standards or false pretences.
7.2 — Medical Protection and Privacy Act (MPPA)
All patients have the legal right to have their sensitive health records protected and not disclosed without proper notification, a legal subpoena, or the patient's consent.
  • Medical records requested by the patient are provided by High Command only.
  • Records requested by any other party must be obtained through a legal subpoena, processed by High Command.

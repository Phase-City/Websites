The Story

Where shadows hold secrets and every corner has a price. Phase City's story is written by the people who live it.

01
Chapter One

The Founding

Phase City wasn't built — it was forged. In the aftermath of San Andreas' economic collapse, a consortium of power brokers, underground kingpins, and visionary politicians carved out a new jurisdiction from the bones of Los Santos. They called it Phase City — a place where reinvention wasn't just possible, it was required. The old rules didn't apply here. The city operated under its own charter, its own law enforcement, its own economy.

02
Chapter Two

The Three Pillars

Phase City runs on three pillars: Order, Commerce, and Shadow. Law enforcement maintains a tenuous grip — enforcing laws that bend but rarely break. Commerce flows through legitimate businesses. And then there's the Shadow — drug operations, weapons trafficking, underground racing circuits, and heist crews that operate with surgical precision. The city needs all three pillars to stand. Remove one, and everything collapses.

03
Chapter Three

The Districts

Downtown Phase City pulses with neon and ambition. South Side is where the streets talk and loyalty is currency. Vinewood Hills is where old money watches the chaos below through tinted glass. Sandy Shores remains lawless in spirit — a frontier where jurisdiction thins. Paleto Bay is quiet, almost too quiet — the kind of place where people go to disappear. Every district has its own rhythm, its own rules, its own legends.

04
Chapter Four

Your Chapter Begins

You arrive in Phase City with nothing but a name and a choice. Put on the badge and hold the line. Build a business empire. Descend into the underworld and make your crew legend. Or walk the razor's edge between all worlds. In Phase City, your story isn't written for you. Every alliance, every betrayal, every bullet and every handshake shapes the living narrative. The moon rises over Phase City tonight. What happens next is up to you.

Core Rules

The foundation of Phase City. Breaking these has consequences — warnings, bans, and faction removal. Know them before you connect.

🚫
No Metagaming

Using out-of-character information in-character is prohibited. What you know as a player, your character does not know. Stream sniping falls under this rule.

🔫
No RDM / VDM

Random Deathmatch and Vehicle Deathmatch are banned. Every violent action requires in-character justification and proper initiation. No murder for no reason.

💀
New Life Rule (NLR)

Upon death you forget the circumstances of that death. You may not return to the area of your death for 15 minutes. You cannot seek revenge for your own death.

🎮
No Fail RP

Act realistically. Your character fears death, values their life, and behaves consistently with the world. Unrealistic actions (suicide cliffs, magic vehicles) are prohibited.

🎙️
Respect In All Channels

Hate speech, harassment, slurs, and targeted abuse are zero-tolerance. Conflict is in-character. OOC disrespect has no place here regardless of what happened in a scene.

🏛️
No Cop Baiting

Deliberately provoking law enforcement without realistic in-character motivation is prohibited. If you want a police chase, build a reason for it through actual RP first.

⚠️
Warning Point System

Violations earn warning points. 5 points = 1-week ban. 10 points = 1-month ban. 20 points = permanent removal. Points expire after 90 days of clean play.

🔒
Gunplay Rules

Full verbal initiation required. No shooting from vehicles without prior initiation. Hostage scenes must follow the hostage RP standard. Surrendering must be honored.

🏥
Combat Logging

Logging out during an active RP scenario is strictly prohibited. If you must disconnect, /me and notify the other players. Staff can review disconnection logs.

Gang Handbook

This handbook governs all whitelisted gang activity on Phase City. It supplements the main server ruleset — it does not replace it.

Part 1
📖 Philosophy
  • Gang RP is about building a living criminal organization with culture, hierarchy, and consequence.
  • Gang RP is NOT a license to shoot. Gangs that consistently produce shoot-first RP get reviewed.
  • Every hostile action must be backed by genuine in-character reasoning. Build the narrative.
Part 2 & 3
📋 Whitelist & Membership
  • Minimum 6 confirmed founding members, named leadership, written lore submission required.
  • All newly whitelisted gangs enter a 14-day probationary period.
  • Rosters must be updated within 48 hours of any membership change.
  • Hangaround period is 7 days minimum before full patch.
  • Gang below 6 active members for 14+ days is subject to whitelist removal.
Part 4 & 5
🏙️ Territory & Colors
  • Territory is a narrative anchor — your home, your block. It is not a mechanical kill zone.
  • Territory must be approved by staff at time of whitelist application.
  • Castle defense: up to 6 members, no additional spotters.
  • Full members must wear identifiable gang colors during gang-representative activities.
  • Impersonating another gang is prohibited.
Part 6 & 7
⚔️ Beef & War
  • Valid beef: repeated robbery, disrespect of leadership, defacing tags, breaking deals.
  • After a scene ends — 5 min no shooting — a 30-minute cooldown begins.
  • New members have a 7-day lockout before participating in active beefs.
  • War requires documented history across at least 3 incidents over 7 days.
  • Max war duration: 14 days. 14-day ceasefire follows resolution.
Part 9 & 10
🎯 Standards & Strikes
  • RP before violence. Gang members are held to the highest initiation standard.
  • A hit cannot be executed on someone with zero prior interaction with your gang.
  • 1 strike = formal warning. 2 strikes = war rights suspended 14 days.
  • 3 strikes = gang whitelist removal. Members revert to unwhitelisted crew.
Part 11
🌐 OOC Standards
  • You are a representative of your gang in every interaction — IC and OOC.
  • Beef stays IC. Conflict between gangs ends when you close the game.
  • Accept the L. Losing a fight or war is a story moment, not a reason to quit.
  • Raise disputes through proper channels after the scene, respectfully.

Best Practices

Not rules — the wisdom that separates good roleplay from legendary roleplay. Read these, live by them, and the city will reward you.

👤
Creating Your Character

Build a character with depth — backstory, motivations, flaws, and goals. Avoid stereotypes and one-dimensional archetypes. Your character should feel like a real person dropped into an extraordinary city.

💬
Initiating Roleplay

Approach others with purpose. Use /me commands and emotes to bring scenes to life. Read the room — if someone is mid-scenario, find a natural way to join. Quality interactions create the stories everyone remembers.

⚡
Handling Conflict

Conflict drives great storytelling, but it must be earned. Build tension through dialogue and escalation before resorting to violence. If you lose or get arrested, roll with it — the best RP comes from consequences.

🏥
Working With Emergency Services

EMS and police are players too. Give them quality scenarios. If downed, stay in character and roleplay injuries. If arrested, engage with the booking process. These interactions are content for everyone.

📈
Growing in the City

Progression takes time. Build relationships, earn trust, climb organically. The best crime bosses started as corner runners. The best chiefs started as cadets. Let your story unfold naturally.

🌟
New Player Tips

Start at the apartments and explore. Visit local businesses, introduce yourself, find your niche. Join Discord to connect with the community. Don't be afraid to ask questions — we all started somewhere.
